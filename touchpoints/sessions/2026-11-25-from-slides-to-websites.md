# From Slides to Websites

- **Speaker:** Kate Astrid
- **Date:** Wednesday, November 25, 2026, 13:00 UTC
- **Format:** Show and tell, 5 to 10 minutes

## Abstract

I'll share how I create presentations as websites instead of traditional slide decks. We'll explore my workflow, and design principles behind turning ideas into engaging, interactive web experiences, and why the web can be a much more flexible canvas for storytelling and presenting.
