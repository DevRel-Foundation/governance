# The Infrastructure Factory

- **Speaker:** Max Körbächer, Liquid Reply
- **Date:** Wednesday, November 11, 2026, 13:00 UTC
- **Format:** Show and tell, 20 to 30 minutes
- **GitHub:** [@mkorbi](https://github.com/mkorbi)

## Abstract

Everyone is building software factories: agents triage, implement, verify and ship. Google reports 75% of new code is AI-generated, Uber attributes over 70% of pull requests to agents. And Anthropic had to reinvent Git to keep up with the speed. But every one of those changes still needs somewhere to run, and in most companies that somewhere still arrives through a ticket.

The problem is that code got cheap. But environments did not.

Let me introduce you the concept of Infrastructure Factory: a platform that turns intents from humans or agents into compliant, owned, observable, cost-attributed environments, with no ticket in the path. We look at why the most-cited software factory talks of 2026 all call infrastructure the hardest part and none treat it as a factory of its own; how it relates to the AI factory (NVIDIA’s kind and the model-factory kind) and where the two share a floor; and what the factory looks like when its front door is an MCP server and its policy gate is agentgateway.
