# AI agents are your new developer audience

- **Speakers:** Waldek Mastykarz and Garry Trinder, Microsoft
- **Date:** Wednesday, December 16, 2026, 13:00 UTC
- **Format:** Show and tell

## Abstract

Developers increasingly rely on AI coding agents to discover technologies, choose dependencies, read documentation, and build applications. That changes how developers experience our products, and it challenges some of the assumptions behind Developer Relations.

We've spent years improving documentation, creating samples, building communities, and helping developers succeed. But what happens when the developer never visits our documentation, and an agent makes technology decisions on their behalf? How do we know whether our efforts still help?

In this session, we'll explore how AI coding agents are changing Developer Relations and introduce Agent Experience (AX): a way to understand and measure how agents discover, select, and use developer technologies. Through real-world examples, we'll see why traditional DevRel activities still matter, where they fall short, and how we can evolve our practices to serve both developers and their agents.

You'll leave with a new perspective on your audience and practical ideas for keeping DevRel relevant in an agent-driven world.
