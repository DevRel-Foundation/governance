# DRF Community Touchpoints

Community Touchpoints are open calls where the DevRel community meets to reconnect, share challenges and work, and decide together where the DevRel Foundation can be most useful

- **When:** every other Wednesday at 13:00 UTC, for 60 minutes. See the [community calendar](https://dev-rel.org/calendar) for your local time and for any changes
- **Who:** everyone. No membership or company affiliation is needed
- **Agenda:** each agenda is posted in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/DevRel-Foundation/governance/discussions/categories/announcements) before the call, and anyone can add topics
- **Format:** a short show and tell from a community member, followed by group Q&A and discussion

Learn more on the [Community Touchpoints page](https://dev-rel.org/community/touchpoints)
